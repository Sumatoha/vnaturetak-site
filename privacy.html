Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

The vnaturetak app ("the app") is a private publishing tool operated by the owner of the vnaturetak channel. It is used only by the channel owner to upload videos the owner created to the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

What data the app uses

The app uses the OAuth access granted by the channel owner (YouTube Data API scope youtube.upload, TikTok video.publish) solely to upload videos and set their title and description. The app does not read, collect, store or share any data about other users, viewers, comments or analytics.

Storage

The only stored data is the owner's OAuth refresh token, kept as an encrypted environment variable on the owner's server. It is never shared with third parties.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or in TikTok app settings.
