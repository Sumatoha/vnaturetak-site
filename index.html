vnaturetak

vnaturetak is a short educational video series: how familiar things really work (brands, money, history), in 30–60 second animated vertical videos in Russian.

The vnaturetak app is an internal publishing tool used only by the channel owner to upload our own finished videos to our own YouTube channel and TikTok account. It does not access, collect or publish data of other users.

Platform: videos are published to our YouTube channel vnaturetak as YouTube Shorts, using the YouTube Data API (YouTube API Services).
